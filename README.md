# Healthcare Executive Dashboard | Power BI / Excel

Healthcare Executive Dashboard - 1.42bn Total Billing, 56K Patients, 25.54K Avg Billing, 22.98% YoY Growth (2019-2024).

## 📊 Project Overview
Analysed healthcare data for 56,000 patients from 2019 to 2024 to understand billing, patient volume, medical conditions, insurance providers and test results. Built an executive dashboard for hospital management decisions.

## 🎯 Key Metrics
- **Total Billing:** 1.42bn
- **Total Patients:** 56K
- **Avg Billing per Patient:** 25.54K
- **YoY Growth %:** 22.98%
- **Period:** 2019 - 2024

## 🔍 Key Findings

**1. Billing grew strongly - 22.98% YoY**
Total billing reached 1.42bn with 22.98% year-on-year growth. Billing peaked in the middle years and the dashboard tracks LY vs Total Billing by Year.

**2. 56K patients, 25.54K average billing**
Average billing of 25.54K per patient shows high-cost care is common - important for pricing and insurance planning.

**3. Insurance drives billing**
Total Billing by Insurance Provider shows Cigna, Blue Cross, Aetna, UnitedHealthcare and Medicare all contribute a large share. No single provider dominates - billing is spread across providers.

**4. Chronic conditions are the biggest drivers**
Total Billing by Medical Condition - Arthritis, Asthma, Cancer, Diabetes, Hypertension and Obesity. Chronic conditions like Cancer, Diabetes and Hypertension drive a large part of billing and patient volume.

**5. Test results are evenly split**
Total Patients by Test Results: Normal ~33%, Abnormal ~33%, Inconclusive ~33%. 1 in 3 patients has an abnormal result - needs follow-up and early intervention.

**6. Billing varies by hospital**
Total Billing by Hospital shows some hospitals bill much more than others - useful for resource allocation and performance review.

## 💡 Recommendations
1. Focus on chronic disease programs - Diabetes, Hypertension, Arthritis, Obesity - prevention will reduce long-term billing
2. Follow up Abnormal + Inconclusive results quickly - 2 in 3 patients are not Normal
3. Review pricing by insurance provider - negotiate better rates with top providers
4. Support high-billing hospitals - check if higher billing = more complex cases or inefficiency
5. Track YoY growth monthly - keep billing growth sustainable while improving patient outcomes
